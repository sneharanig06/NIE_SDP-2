College Introduction

The National Institute of Engineering (NIE), Mysuru is a reputed engineering institution in Karnataka. It provides quality education and encourages innovation, technical skills and sustainable practices.

Green Campus Activities

NIE promotes a clean and green campus through various eco-friendly activities. CREST, a campus organization, promotes renewable energy and sustainable practices.

  • Tree plantation
  • Energy conservation
  • Water conservation
  • Eco-friendly activities

Solar Energy

Solar energy is a clean and renewable source of energy. It helps reduce the use of conventional energy and supports a sustainable campus.

Waste Management

Proper waste management helps keep the campus clean and healthy. Waste reduction, reuse and recycling are important parts of sustainable campus practices.
